Walks & prices

Five walks, one price each.

Every walk starts and ends at your door, a local bar or the groomer. Prices are per walk, one dog.

  • Town

    30 min

    €14

    A brisk sniff around Blessington town and its green edges. Good for older dogs, puppies and lunchtime leg-stretches.

  • Lake

    1 hour

    €20

    Down to the water and along the shore paths of Blessington Lakes. Open space, big smells and a full hour on the move.

  • Forest

    90 min

    €27

    Into the big forest behind the town and back by the lake. Shade in summer, mud in winter, a happy dog either way.

  • Long loop

    2 hours

    €34

    The full ramble for dogs with energy to burn: lake shore, woodland and fields, at whatever pace suits your dog.

  • Greenway to Russborough

    About 90 min each way

    €27 one way · €34 return

    Along the Blessington Greenway to Russborough House. The sign says 6.5 km and about an hour; with dogs it is nearer 90 minutes each way. Dogs stay on the lead. We walk to the house, not inside it.

Where we walk

Blessington and nearby. Nowhere else.

  • Blessington town and the green around it
  • Blessington Lakes (Poulaphouca Reservoir) shore paths
  • Blessington Greenway
  • Glen Ding forest behind the town
  • Russborough House, by the Greenway

Owners choose the path. If your dog has a favourite loop, tell us and that is the walk.

Dogs

Small dogs, big dogs, bouncy dogs, slow dogs.

Walks are kept small so every dog gets attention: up to three small dogs together, or one or two bigger dogs. Never a pack.

Dog photos are licensed photographs, credited in the footer — not client dogs.

How it works

Four steps, no fuss.

  1. Say hello

    Tell us about your dog, where you are in Blessington and which walk you fancy.

  2. Quick meet

    We meet you and your dog first, on the lead, no rush. If it is not a fit, no bother.

  3. The walk

    Your chosen route, in small numbers. Dogs stay on the lead on the Greenway. If you would like your dog to wear a tracker tag on the walk, just ask.

  4. Back to you

    Collection and drop-off at home, a local bar or the groomer. Pay by cash or Revolut.

Weekday afternoons and weekends.
